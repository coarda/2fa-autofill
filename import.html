1. Show the export QR code on your phone

  1. Open Google Authenticator on your phone.
  2. Tap the ☰ menu (or ⋮) → Transfer accounts → Export accounts.
  3. Select the accounts you want and tap Next. A QR code appears. If you picked many accounts there may be several QR codes. Import each one.

Your phone won't allow screenshots of that screen, so the easiest way is to hold the phone up to your webcam. You can also take a photo of the phone screen with another device and upload it.
